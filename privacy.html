Privacy Policy

Last updated October 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information JINY LLC, a North Carolina limited liability company ("JINY," "we," "us," or "our"), collects when you visit babystepstothemic.com, join our email list, or buy and take part in the 6-Week Articulation Bootcamp by Baby Steps to the Mic (the "Program"), and what we do with it.

The short version: we collect what we need to run the Program and stay in touch with you, we don't sell your information, and you can ask us to delete it.

1. Information We Collect

Information you give us:

  • At checkout: your name, email address, billing address, the payment plan you choose, and the date and time you agreed to our terms.
  • Payment details: your card or other payment information is entered directly into a form run by Stripe, our payment processor. We never see or store your full card number. We receive a record of the payment, such as the amount, its status, and a payment reference.
  • Email list: your email address, and your name if you provide it, when you join our waitlist or mailing list, including through a message or comment on Instagram.
  • In the Program: your Circle profile, the recordings of yourself speaking that you choose to post, and your posts, comments, and messages.
  • On live calls: your name, voice, and image if you take part in a Zoom session, which may be recorded.
  • When you contact us: whatever you include in your email or message.

Information collected automatically:

  • Page views: we use Vercel Web Analytics to count visits to our pages. It does not use cookies and does not identify you personally.
  • Technical data: like most websites, our hosting and service providers receive standard technical information such as your IP address, browser type, and device type.
  • Email activity: our email provider tells us whether an email was delivered, opened, or clicked, and whether you unsubscribed.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • to process your payment and keep a record of your order;
  • to give you access to the Program, place you in a cohort, and run the community and live sessions;
  • to send you Program emails, such as your welcome email, schedule updates, and announcements;
  • to send you marketing emails about the Program and related offers, which you can unsubscribe from at any time;
  • to answer your questions and provide support;
  • to understand how our site is used and improve it;
  • to prevent fraud and abuse, enforce our Program Terms, and meet our legal, tax, and accounting obligations.

How we may use content you share in the Program, including recordings of you speaking, is covered in our Program Terms. We will not use recordings of you speaking, or your full name, face, or voice, in public marketing without asking your permission first.

3. Who We Share It With

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with advertisers. We share it only with the companies that help us run the Program, and only what each one needs:

  • Stripe processes payments.
  • Supabase stores our order and email-list records.
  • EmailOctopus sends our emails.
  • Circle hosts the private community and course materials.
  • Zoom hosts live sessions and their recordings.
  • Vercel hosts this website and provides page-view analytics.
  • Meta (Instagram) and automated messaging tools we use with it, if you message us or comment on Instagram.

Each of these companies handles your information under its own privacy policy. What you post in the community is visible to other members of the Program.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it, to protect our rights or the safety of others, or as part of a sale or transfer of our business.

4. Cookies

We do not use advertising or tracking cookies on this website. Stripe sets cookies on our checkout page to process payments and prevent fraud. Circle and Zoom use their own cookies when you use those services.

5. How Long We Keep It

We keep your information for as long as we need it for the purposes above. Order and payment records are kept for as long as tax and accounting rules require. We keep you on our email list until you unsubscribe or ask us to remove you. Content you post in the community stays there until you or we delete it, or the community is closed.

6. Your Choices & Rights

  • Marketing emails: use the unsubscribe link in any email. We may still send you essential messages about a Program you purchased.
  • Your content: you can edit or delete your posts and recordings in Circle.
  • Access, correction, and deletion: email team@babystepstothemic.com to ask for a copy of the information we hold about you, to correct it, or to have it deleted. We may need to keep some records, such as proof of a purchase, where the law requires it.

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under local privacy law, such as the right to object to certain uses of your information or to complain to your data protection authority. We will honor requests that apply to you.

7. Where Your Information Is Stored

We are based in the United States, and our service providers store and process information in the United States and other countries. If you are outside the United States, your information will be transferred to and processed in countries whose privacy laws may differ from those where you live.

8. Security

We use reputable providers and reasonable safeguards to protect your information, including encrypted connections and limited access to our records. No method of storing or sending information is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Children

This website and the Program are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has given us personal information, email team@babystepstothemic.com and we will delete it.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes take effect when posted on this page, and we will update the date at the top. We will notify current members of material changes by email.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your information? Email us at team@babystepstothemic.com.
