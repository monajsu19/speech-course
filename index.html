the roadmap

  1. pronunciation drills+ daily 1 min speaking practicewarm up your mouth muscles for clean sounds and gain awareness of which syllables you mumble
  2. rhythm, pitch & volume drills+ daily 1 min speaking practiceadd vocal variety with rhythm in your speech. vary your speed of speech and change your volume
  3. tone & pausing drills+ daily 1 min speaking practicelearn how to use your voice to elicit different emotions and how to pause effectively
  4. shadowing drills+ daily 1 min speaking practicecopy a speaker you admire: their pace, their energy, the way they land a point. this was the first exercise I did on my page, and it’s quite helpful
  5. stage presence and audience engagement tips+ daily 1 min speaking practicewe’ll work on how to look and feel confident when public speaking, get over the fear of being judged, and speak in a way that keeps the audience’s attention
  6. storytelling & humor tips+ daily 1 min speaking practiceI’ll teach you what I know about keeping an audience engaged, plus a few humor tips I learned in my (very short) stand-up comedy endeavor

what if I'm really, really shy?

I was really shy when I first started this whole journey! I could barely get my own name out at my first Toastmasters meeting. everyone here is trying to get better. you're in the right place.

what happens in a sunday live session?

we all hop on a big zoom call and practice public speaking together: speaking games, drills, and fun debate rounds. it's your chance to practice getting over your nerves with basically no stakes.

do I have to post my videos?

no, you don't, but it's strongly encouraged! every saturday, you can post the video of your 1-minute speech in the chat, and everyone gives each other feedback. one of the best ways to learn is from watching other people.

how much time does this take?

10 to 20 minutes a day:

  • monday – friday: watch my video tip, do the drill, record yourself, speak for 1 minute.
  • saturday: speak for 1 minute and post it in the chat for feedback.
  • every other sunday: we meet live on zoom (3 sessions total).

what if I miss a day?

don't quit. just do the next one. every rep still counts.
