where I started

last year, I gave myself the new year's resolution to get over my fear of public speaking. I was too nervous to speak up in meetings, had some social anxiety, and it really started to affect my daily life.

what changed

I found a local Toastmasters club and committed to going every week. I made an instagram to track my progress: every drill I was playing around with to reduce filler words, improve my pronunciation, expand my vocab. anything I could get my hands on, I gave it a shot. andddd somewhere along the way, 300k people across platforms started following along!

here's what I realized

no one ever really teaches us how to speak. there's no class in school that trains it, but it's literally one of the most important skills. there are so many stats out there about how communication determines your promotion, how people perceive you, how happy you are... I mean, basically everything.
